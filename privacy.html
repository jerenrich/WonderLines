Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Wonder Lines is an iPhone and iPad app that turns an idea into a coloring sheet. It is for families; children should use it with a parent or guardian. It is operated by Jordan Erenrich. This policy describes the app and its generation service, and is written with parents and guardians in mind because children may use it.

The short version

  • Wonder Lines does not ask for a child's name, email address, phone number, photo or location, and there is no sign-in.
  • Descriptions are screened for personal details, such as a full name, address, school or phone number, by an automated filter, and rejected when it finds them. The filter can miss things, so please remind children to leave personal details out.
  • A grown-up must answer a question and agree before the first sheet is made.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics software. Information is never sold.
  • Generated images are deleted from the service after 24 hours. The anonymous account and its records are deleted after 180 days without use, or at any time from Settings.

A grown-up's permission before the first sheet

Before the first sheet, the app asks a grown-up to answer a multiplication question, then shows a notice explaining what is sent to which services. Nothing is sent for generation until the grown-up taps Agree. Cancel sends nothing. The choice is saved on the device. A grown-up can withdraw it in Settings, which pauses new generation and recovery but cannot recall a request already sent. A material change to the sharing described here will require a new choice.

Information we collect

  • Descriptions. When Generate is tapped, the description, drawing-difficulty setting, selected model and image size are sent to the Wonder Lines service. Each description is checked first. Descriptions that appear to include identifying or contact details of a real person, such as a full name, home address, school name, phone number, email address or username, are rejected when the automated filter finds them. The filter can miss some details or occasionally reject a harmless description. A first name on its own, family words like "grandma", pet names and story characters are allowed.
  • Generated images. The service keeps each generated image for 24 hours so an interrupted sheet can be recovered, then deletes it.
  • An anonymous account and device verification. The app creates a random account automatically, without a name, email or sign-in. The service stores the account identifier, a credential identifier and records of each generation. On supported devices, Apple's App Attest checks that requests come from the genuine app; the service stores the related key identifier, public key and counters. These identifiers are used only to run the service: to authenticate requests, apply daily limits, prevent abuse and recover sheets. They link activity from the same installation but do not identify who the child is.
  • Usage and technical data. The service records generation counts, request identifiers, model choices, times, status and error codes, and performance and cost measurements. Cloudflare, which hosts the service, processes connection information, including IP addresses.

The app does not request access to contacts, location, the microphone, the camera or existing photos, and does not use an advertising identifier.

How information is used

Descriptions are used to create coloring sheets and to screen them for unsuitable content and personal details. Identifiers, usage records and technical data are used to authenticate requests, apply service limits, prevent abuse and duplicate generation, recover results, keep the service running and investigate failures. Wonder Lines does not use information for advertising, does not track children across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell information.

Who receives information

The service runs on Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects and R2, and Cloudflare AI Gateway routes generation requests. Gateway request logging is disabled for generation, recovery and safety checks, and descriptions are not written to the service's diagnostic logs.

Each description is checked by two safety services before a sheet is made: Cloudflare's Clef, run through Cloudflare Workers AI, and TypeSafe AI's Jev. They screen for unsuitable content and for personal details. Like any automated filter, they can miss things or occasionally reject a harmless description, and AI-generated sheets can sometimes include something unexpected; Report sheet lets you flag one. A sheet is made only if neither service rejects the description; if one service is unavailable or does not respond within a few seconds, the other service's decision is used. TypeSafe receives only the description. The service records moderation scores, request identifiers, timing and fixed failure codes to investigate safety decisions, but not the descriptions themselves.

Sheets are drawn by OpenAI, or by Cloudflare Workers AI or fal.ai if one of their models is offered and selected. Requests to these image models and to the safety services contain only the description, drawing guidance and image settings. They do not include the anonymous account identifier, device-verification data or an IP address, because they are sent by the Wonder Lines service, not the device. These providers may keep request logs under their own terms.

For more information, see Cloudflare's privacy policy, OpenAI's privacy policy, fal.ai's privacy policy, TypeSafe AI's service policies and Apple's privacy policy. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Information on the device

The drawing-difficulty, model and number-of-sheets settings are saved on the device. The account credential is stored in the iOS Keychain. The app keeps the current description, the gallery of sheets and unfinished-generation identifiers so they survive interruptions. It also keeps a diagnostics history of request identifiers and status codes, without descriptions, images or credentials, which is shared only if someone chooses to share it.

Saving a sheet asks for permission to add it to Photos; the app never reads existing photos. Sharing and printing send the chosen sheet to the destination selected through Apple's standard controls.

Reporting a sheet

Report sheet in the sheet actions flags an inappropriate result. The app sends the sheet's service identifier to Wonder Lines support, who can review the generation details, and the image while it is still stored, to remove unsuitable output and improve the safety checks. A reported sheet is hidden from the gallery and cannot be recovered. Reports are kept privately with the anonymous account until reviewed and removed, or until the account is deleted.

Account safety

Twenty content-policy rejections within 24 hours suspend new generation for that account for 24 hours. Each description counts once, including its related sheets and retries. Rejections only for personal details, safety-service outages and uncertain results do not count. Support can also suspend or reinstate an account after review. A suspension does not prevent reporting, contacting support or deleting the account.

To apply this rule, the service keeps rejected-submission identifiers, opaque fingerprints and times for 24 hours, without the rejected descriptions. It keeps the current suspension for its duration and up to 50 suspension or reinstatement changes for 90 days, recording the anonymous account identifier, action, time, expiry and any support reason. These records are removed when the account is deleted.

How long information is kept

  • Descriptions: kept with a sheet's job record only until the sheet finishes or fails.
  • Generated images: 24 hours on the service. Copies saved to Photos, shared or printed stay where they were sent.
  • The anonymous account, its generation records, reports and safety records: deleted automatically after 180 days without any use of the app's online features, or straight away using Delete account and data.
  • Service diagnostic logs: up to seven days.
  • After deletion, a marker containing the random account identifier, a credential hash and the deletion time is kept for about 24 hours to block late requests. Service-wide daily totals are not reset.
  • Records kept by Cloudflare, OpenAI, TypeSafe AI, fal.ai and Apple follow their own retention terms and are not erased by deleting the account.

Removing the app does not delete the account on the service, and iOS may keep the Keychain credential, but the account is deleted after 180 days without use.

Choices for parents and guardians

  • Delete everything: Settings, then Delete account and data. This removes the anonymous account, its records, stored images and reports, and clears the app's gallery, settings, diagnostics, credential and consent choice. No email or sign-in is needed. If the connection fails, the app pauses generation and offers Retry.
  • Withdraw permission: Settings, then Withdraw AI consent.
  • Ask a question or request deletion: email the address below. Because accounts are anonymous, a request identifier from Settings, then Diagnostics, helps locate the records. Please do not send access tokens or a child's personal information.
