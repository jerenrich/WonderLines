Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Wonder Lines is an iPhone and iPad app operated by Jordan Erenrich. It turns text descriptions into coloring sheets using a hosted service and AI image providers. This policy describes the app and its generation service.

Your choice before AI generation

Starting with version 1.0 build 6, the app asks for permission before sending information for AI generation or recovery. The prompt identifies the information shared and the providers involved. Your choice is saved on your device, so you do not have to agree for every sheet. Cancel leaves your description in place and sends no generation request. You can withdraw permission in Settings; this pauses new generation and recovery but cannot recall a request already sent. A material change to the sharing described here will require a new choice.

Information processed by the service

  • Descriptions and generated images. When you tap Generate, your description, drawing-complexity instructions, selected model, and requested image dimensions are sent to the generation service and the provider used for that model. Generated images are stored by the service so interrupted requests can be recovered.
  • Anonymous account and device identifiers. The app creates a random service account without asking for a name, email address, or sign-in. The service stores the account identifier, credential identifier, and generation records. Apple's App Attest is used for device verification on supported devices; the service stores the associated key identifier, public key, and verification counters. These identifiers can link activity from the same installation even though you have not provided a name.
  • Usage and technical data. The service records generation counts, request and job identifiers, model choices, timestamps, status and error codes, and performance and cost metrics. Hosting and AI providers may process connection information, including IP addresses, and retain request or response logs under their own policies.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or existing photo library. Please avoid entering names, contact details, or other sensitive information in descriptions.

Why information is used

Descriptions and images are used to create and return coloring sheets. Descriptions are also checked for suitability for all ages before generation. Identifiers, usage records, and technical data are used to authenticate requests, apply service limits, prevent abuse and duplicate generation, recover results, operate the service, and investigate failures.

Wonder Lines contains no advertising SDK and does not use an advertising identifier. The app and its backend do not sell data or use it for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

Service providers

The backend runs on Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, and R2. Cloudflare AI Gateway routes generation requests. The service update on 17 October 2026 disables Gateway request logging for generation, recovery and moderation and stops writing descriptions to Worker diagnostic logs. New sheets in build 7 use OpenAI or Cloudflare Workers AI, depending on the selected model. Earlier versions also offered fal.ai; recovery and technical usage lookups for those existing sheets may still use fal.ai. Provider-operated models may have their own terms and data practices. If a model route changes, the provider used for that route can change.

Descriptions are checked through Cloudflare Workers AI and AI Gateway before a new coloring sheet is generated. The current moderation model is Cloudflare Clef; configured alternatives include Clef-flash and TypeSafe AI's Jev. Updated app versions send the original description for moderation, while older versions may include drawing guidance. The service records moderation scores when available, request identifiers, policy information, timing and fixed failure codes to investigate safety decisions and service failures. Descriptions and moderation request or response bodies are excluded from these diagnostic logs. Descriptions and moderation scores are not included in the app's shared diagnostic reports.

Provider retention and any provider use of content are governed by the applicable service terms and account settings. Wonder Lines does not promise that generation requests are processed without provider logging.

For more information, see Cloudflare's privacy policy, OpenAI's privacy policy, fal.ai's privacy policy, and Apple's privacy policy. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Information on your device

Age difficulty, model choice, and image-count preferences are saved locally. The numeric age setting is sent with updated app requests so the service can apply drawing-complexity guidance; older app versions send that guidance with the description. Service credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. The app saves your description draft, generated gallery, selected sheet, and unfinished-generation identifiers locally so they can survive interruption or relaunch. It also keeps a local diagnostics history with request identifiers and technical status information, excluding descriptions, image bytes, and credentials.

Saving a sheet requests permission to add that image to Photos. The app does not read your existing photos. Sharing and printing send the selected image to the destination you choose through Apple's system controls. Those destinations handle the image under their own practices. A diagnostics report is shared only when you choose to share it.

Reporting a sheet

In build 6 and later, use Report sheet in the sheet actions to flag an inappropriate result. The app sends the sheet's service identifier and your selected reason to Wonder Lines support. Support can inspect the saved image and generation details to investigate, remove inappropriate output, and improve safety checks. A successful report hides that sheet from your gallery and prevents it being recovered. Reports are stored privately with the associated account until reviewed and removed, or until you delete the account. You can also contact support by email.

Retention and deletion

The backend does not currently enforce a fixed automatic deletion period for anonymous accounts, completed images, or generation and usage records. Pending job descriptions are removed from the account job record when processing becomes terminal. The service update on 17 October 2026 disables new Gateway request logs and excludes descriptions from new Worker diagnostic logs. Historical Gateway logs recorded before logging was disabled were deleted on 17 October 2026. New Gateway request logging remains disabled. Cloudflare Worker diagnostic logs expire within seven days. Duplicate usage-diagnostic files are no longer written to service storage; a scheduled hourly cleanup removes older copies more than seven days after their last write. This diagnostic cleanup does not remove saved sheets or reports. Independent AI provider processing and retention follow the provider’s applicable service terms and account settings.

In build 6 and later, open Settings and choose Delete account and data. After confirmation, the app stops waiting for active generation and requests deletion of your anonymous service account, authentication and device-verification records, server images, generation and usage records, and reports. Once the service confirms deletion, the app clears its local gallery, draft, preferences, diagnostics, recovery references, credentials, and consent choice. You do not need to email support or create a named account. If the connection fails, the app keeps the credential needed to retry, pauses generation, and offers Retry. The server also retries interrupted cleanup.

A temporary deletion marker containing the random account identifier, a credential hash, and deletion time remains for approximately 24 hours to block and clean up late requests. Aggregate service-wide usage totals are not reset. Cloudflare Worker diagnostic records may remain for up to seven days, including descriptions recorded before the 17 October 2026 service update. New Wonder Lines diagnostic records exclude descriptions and generated image bodies. Historical Gateway logs were deleted on 17 October 2026, as described above. Other provider connection and diagnostic records follow the provider’s applicable retention policies. Independent AI provider logs are governed by their service terms and are not erased by this in-app action. Copies exported to Photos, shared elsewhere, or printed remain at those destinations.

Removing the app does not automatically delete server records, and iOS may retain Keychain credentials. Images you save or share remain in the destinations you selected. For questions or a request to remove information held by Wonder Lines, contact the address below. Because accounts are anonymous, it may be necessary to identify the relevant account or generation before records can be located. Do not send access tokens or other credentials.

Family use

Wonder Lines supports family coloring activities. Parents and guardians should supervise children's descriptions and sharing, avoid entering children's personal information, and review generated sheets before use. The drawing-difficulty slider is not an age-verification or parental-control system. AI results can vary.
