About Wonder Lines

Make room for imagination.

Wonder Lines was created by Jordan Erenrich for creative coloring activities, classrooms, and time together. Describe a scene, choose the drawing difficulty, and generate a page with room to color.

Generation is free for users, subject to service availability and daily limits. The app connects to the hosted generation service automatically; no sign-in, API key, or server setup is needed.

Help and safety

Support

For help with Wonder Lines, email jordan.erenrich@gmail.com. Include your app version, device, and what happened. Please do not send access tokens, passwords, or children's personal information.

Use Report sheet in the sheet actions to report an inappropriate result. Settings includes the privacy policy, a choice to withdraw AI consent, and Delete account and data. If deletion is interrupted, reconnect and use Retry.

See the privacy policy for information about AI providers, stored data, and deletion.
