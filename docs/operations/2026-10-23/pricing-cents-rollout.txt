The Workers AI neuron dataset cannot establish externally hosted OpenAI/fal
image charges. Their provider token/billing evidence and estimates are retained.
Gateway zero cost attribution must not be interpreted as free generation.
Moderation is still excluded from image estimates. Clef matches 24 cents/M
input tokens. Clef Flash has historical 9 cents/M rows and recent 3.8 cents/M
rows; historical usage must not be repriced using today's list rate.

All app and comparison-site price displays use US cents with the ¢ prefix:
totals, per-image details, provider unit prices and historical explanations.
Up to eight fractional cent digits preserve tiny nonzero costs. Amounts below
the display precision use a less-than indicator, never an apparent zero price.
Stored/API *Usd values retain USD units; conversion happens at presentation.
Comparison exports use *Cents field names and priceUnit = US cents.
The native app refreshes estimates for all saved gallery providers when Usage
opens or refreshes, so historical local costs receive server corrections too.
Reads retain usable saved estimates on failed or incomplete lookups and do not
submit paid generation requests.

No paid generations were submitted for this work. Gross estimates exclude free
allowances, account credits, moderation and infrastructure; they are not invoices.
Deployment, app installation and final validation are recorded in
pricing-cents-deployment-verification.json.
