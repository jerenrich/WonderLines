## App Information

- **Category:** Kids, age band **6–8**. Apple requires Kids to be the primary or secondary category; keep Graphics & Design as the other if you want it.
- **Age rating questionnaire:** answer **Made for Kids** with the **6–8** age band in the final step.
- **Content questions:** Wonder Lines now rejects violence, weapons, horror and frightening content before generation (moderation policy `all-ages-v12-veto-personal`). If you are comfortable that this is reliable, answer **None** for Cartoon or Fantasy Violence, Horror/Fear Themes and Guns or Other Weapons, which were previously **Infrequent**. Keep every other answer as submitted: no user-generated content (sheets are private), no messaging, no advertising, no web access, no gambling, no medical content.

**Name:** Wonder Lines

**Promotional text (170):**
A dinosaur riding a bike on the moon? Kids describe an idea and Wonder Lines draws a coloring page to save, share or print. No sign-in, no ads.

**Description:**
Turn a child's imagination into a page to color.

A dinosaur riding a bike on the moon. A dragon baking cupcakes. Whatever your child dreams up, Wonder Lines turns their description into a coloring sheet on iPhone and iPad, ready to print.

DESCRIBE AN IDEA
Type a scene or character, or tap Surprise me for a starter idea. Choose a drawing difficulty for simpler shapes or finer details.

PICK A FAVORITE
Wonder Lines draws up to three sheets at a time. Swipe through them and choose the one to color.

PRINT AND COLOR
Save a sheet to Photos, share it, or print it with an AirPrint printer.

MADE FOR KIDS, SET UP BY GROWN-UPS
- A grown-up answers a quick question and agrees to how descriptions are used before the first sheet.
- No sign-in, no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
- Every description is checked before drawing. Unsuitable ideas, and personal details such as full names or addresses, are turned away.
- Sheets are private. There is no feed, chat or sharing with other users.
- Delete everything at any time from Settings.

An internet connection is needed to make new sheets. Wonder Lines uses AI image generation, so results vary.
