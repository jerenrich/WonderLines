Hello App Review team,

Please review Wonder Lines 1.0 (build 10).

Build 8 was rejected under guideline 2.1(a) after a generation error. We traced it to our description safety check: the Cloudflare-hosted moderation service did not respond within our eight-second limit, so image generation never started.

Descriptions are now checked by two independent services in parallel, Cloudflare Clef and TypeSafe Jev. A rejection from either service blocks generation. If one service is slow or unavailable, generation proceeds on the other service's approval after a short wait, so a single service outage no longer blocks image generation.

Build 10 names TypeSafe AI in the AI sharing notice and asks for consent again before generation. The public privacy policy has been updated to match.

Safety controls:
- Every description is checked before any image is generated.
- Report sheet (the flag button above the sheet; on narrower screens such as iPhone, in the Sheet actions menu) sends a private report to support and removes that sheet from the gallery.
- Accounts with 20 content-policy rejections in 24 hours are automatically suspended from generation for 24 hours. We can also suspend or reinstate an account after review. Suspension is enforced on the server; reporting, support and account deletion remain available.
- Generated sheets are private. There is no public gallery, feed, messaging or other users' content.

To test:
1. Open Wonder Lines with an internet connection.
2. Enter "A friendly dinosaur riding a bicycle on the moon".
3. Tap Generate and then Agree and Generate on the AI sharing notice.
4. Wait for the three sheets, then swipe or use the arrows to view them.

No sign-in, purchase or settings change is required. Generation is free for users, subject to service availability and a daily allowance of 100 images per anonymous account. Settings includes consent withdrawal, account and data deletion, the privacy policy, and support.
