Wonder Lines 1.0 (5) — additional App Review audit
17 October 2026

Scope
Reviewed the submitted archive, current Swift and Worker source, live App Store
Connect listing/privacy/platform declarations, public privacy/support pages,
Apple's current review guidance, and focused iPad simulator flows.
Build 5 remains Waiting for Review. No new reply was sent, no submission or
distribution setting was changed, and no paid generation was requested.

HIGH PRIORITY — confirmed implementation gaps

1. No privacy policy accessible inside the app.
   Settings contains Age difficulty, Generation and Help -> Diagnostics only.
   The website and App Store privacy URL are present, but neither provides the
   in-app link Apple also requires. Add Privacy Policy and Contact Support in
   Settings.
   Evidence: ColoringSheets/Views/ContentView.swift:443-501, especially 487.
   Rule: App Review 5.1.1(i).
   https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy

2. No explicit disclosure/permission before sending descriptions to AI services.
   Generate starts the remote request directly. There is no consent state or
   first-use disclosure. Descriptions may contain personal information, and the
   service processes them through Cloudflare and the selected image provider
   (OpenAI, Cloudflare Workers AI or fal.ai), with possible logging. Model labels
   and a policy on a separate website do not supply explicit in-app permission.
   Add a clear first-use disclosure and choice before sending a description,
   naming the data and providers, with policy access and a withdrawal option.
   Evidence: ContentView.swift:281; Core/Generation.swift:436; privacy.html:28-30.
   Rule: App Review 5.1.2(i), including its explicit third-party AI provision.
   https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#data-use-and-sharing

3. Automatically created service accounts cannot be deleted inside the app.
   Guest credentials and persistent server account/job/image data are created,
   but there is no deletion control or API. The local credential remove helper
   is not a complete account deletion flow. The privacy policy instead directs
   users to email. Apple explicitly includes automatically created guest accounts
   in its requirement and disallows email-only deletion for ordinary apps.
   Add authenticated deletion of the service account and associated data, with
   confirmation and safe handling of in-flight jobs, then clear local state.
   Evidence: Core/AnonymousIdentity.swift:25-40,78; worker src/index.mjs:531-601;
   privacy.html:35-37. Paths are relative to the project except the Worker source,
   which is under workers/coloring-sheets-api/.
   Rule: App Review 5.1.1(v).
   https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion

4. The actual build-5 archive contains no PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest.
   The app uses UserDefaults for preferences, saved state and diagnostics.
   Apple's required-reason API declaration applies to the app's own API use.
   Add and bundle the manifest with NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults and
   the applicable approved reason (CA92.1 for this app's private preferences),
   then verify the exported archive actually contains it. This is separate from
   the published App Privacy labels. Apple accepted build 5 for processing, so
   this finding does not imply that Apple has already blocked the build.
   Evidence: Core/ColoringViewModel.swift:93; Core/DiagnosticLog.swift:42;
   Scripts/create_project.py:21; zero .xcprivacy files inside the submitted app.
   https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/describing-use-of-required-reason-api
   https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/app-privacy-configuration/nsprivacyaccessedapitypes/nsprivacyaccessedapitype

ADDITIONAL CONTENT-SAFETY RISK

5. No useful in-app route to report an inappropriate generated sheet.
   Prompt moderation exists, but gallery actions only export or show usage;
   there is no Report Sheet or accessible support action. Current guideline 1.2
   explicitly includes AI content creation apps. Add reporting with a generation
   identifier and a defined response/removal process. A user-blocking UI is not
   automatically appropriate here because no other users' content is displayed.
   Evidence: ContentView.swift:111-146,443-501; review-notes.txt:8.
   https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content

LOWER PRIORITY

- Usage/error text says users may be charged even though generation is free and
  there are no purchases. Clarify that displayed amounts are developer-paid
  service costs. ContentView.swift:541; Generation.swift:248-250.
- The selectable Redmond model is labeled Beta. Avoid presenting an unfinished
  feature in the shipping app; remove that label only if the feature is ready,
  otherwise omit it from the release. Generation.swift:25.
- Mac availability remains enabled for macOS 14+, with compatibility unverified;
  Vision Pro availability is also enabled. Neither was runtime-tested here.
  App Attest is NOT a confirmed Mac blocker: a read-only check of deployed Worker
  version 98b17f08-5041-490e-b461-4bdf76795a84 found APP_ATTEST_ENFORCE=false.

CHECKS THAT DID NOT PRODUCE A FINDING

- Current privacy labels declare linked user content, IDs, usage and diagnostics.
  They do not incorrectly claim Data Not Collected.
- The live description correctly says 1-3 sheets, not the older draft's 1-5.
- The support and privacy website responds successfully.
- Current rating is 9+ with regional exceptions; Graphics & Design category.
  A drawing-difficulty slider is not age verification, but this alone does not
  prove a Kids Category violation. The private gallery also does not by itself
  establish an incorrect UGC answer in Apple's age-rating questionnaire.
- Four iPad Air 11-inch (M3), iPadOS 27.0 simulator checks passed: settings,
  portrait/landscape editing, largest accessibility text, and gallery navigation.
- Offline Worker, batch moderation and configuration tests passed.

Runtime export check and final evidence are recorded in audit-verification.json.
Simulator generation uses mock images; no live AI quality/safety sampling was
performed during this audit. The earlier physical-device generation check is
recorded separately in verification.json.

Recommendation
Address the privacy, consent, deletion, manifest and reporting gaps together in
the next build, with matching backend support and revised privacy/review notes.
These are independent of the fixed first-launch Generate issue. A new binary
would require replacing build 5 in the review submission; this audit did not do
that or send further messages to Apple.
