Wonder Lines 1.0 (6) — implementation and verification
17 October 2026

Five targeted findings addressed
1. Settings links to the public privacy policy and support section, plus email.
   Updated policy/support pages are published and verified accessible.
2. AI sharing permission appears before the first generation or recovery and is
   saved across relaunches. Cancel preserves the draft and sends no generation.
   Settings can withdraw consent. A materially changed disclosure version asks
   again; an ordinary app update does not.
3. Account deletion removes the anonymous account, attestation record, jobs,
   images, reports and account-linked budget claims, then local gallery, draft,
   preferences, diagnostics, recovery references and credentials. Failed cleanup
   retains the retry credential and pauses generation with a visible Retry path.
   In-flight work drains or is rejected, preventing deleted images reappearing.
   A temporary 24-hour deletion marker and aggregate service totals remain;
   external/hosting logs and exported images follow the published policy.
4. PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy is in both the archive and exported IPA, with the
   UserDefaults approved reason CA92.1 and matching collected-data categories.
5. Report sheet sends an authenticated reason/reference to a private R2 queue.
   Confirmed reports remove the sheet from gallery and block recovery. Cross-
   account access is rejected. Human review is required: see build6-report-
   operations.txt for the daily Cloudflare review/removal procedure. No automatic
   email notification has been configured.

Verification
- Full iPad unit suite: 81 tests, 77 passed, 4 opt-in live tests skipped, 0 failures.
- Five focused UI flows per device (10 total) passed across iPad Air 11-inch M3
  and iPhone 17e simulators, OS 27.0: consent/withdrawal/deletion, reporting,
  accessibility text, rotation/gallery/editing, and settings navigation.
- New privacy backend suite and existing Worker, batch moderation, moderation,
  and moderation-lab suites passed without paid requests.
- One separate live generation was submitted, succeeded, reported, made
  unrecoverable, and deleted with its temporary account/report. Deleted access
  was rejected; a second deletion returned success. No real user data was used.
- Release archive and local App Store export succeeded. IPA uses live service,
  build 6, production App Attest and distribution signature (debugging disabled).
- Website publication and server CI/deployment passed for commit 2018c17.

Test adjustments and limits
The settings test was updated to scroll to Diagnostics after the new privacy
section. One iPhone clear-field test initially tapped during the keyboard's
entrance animation; the recording showed the target moving. It passed in an
isolated rerun. The test now waits until the keyboard is fully on screen before
that tap. This was a test timing change; app behavior was not changed for it.
Simulated images exercise the UI, and the separate live check exercises the
server. Build 6 has not been tested on a physical device, Mac or Vision Pro.
Lower-priority audit observations (cost wording, Beta label and untested extra
platform availability) are outside the five targeted fixes and remain open.
