We found and fixed the issue. On a fresh installation, Generate stayed disabled until an age difficulty was selected in Settings.

Build 1.0 (5) now uses a default difficulty, so you can enter a description and tap Generate immediately. You can still change the difficulty in Settings.

We reproduced the issue on an iPad Air 11-inch (M3) simulator running iPadOS 27.0 and verified the fix. We also tested iPhone layouts and successfully generated a sheet on a physical iPad.

Please review the updated build 1.0 (5).

Thank you.
