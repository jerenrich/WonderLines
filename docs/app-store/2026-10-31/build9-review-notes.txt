Hello App Review team,

Please review Wonder Lines 1.0 (build 9).

We investigated the latest generation failure and identified a moderation-service availability issue. The Cloudflare-hosted content safety check did not return within our eight-second timeout, preventing image generation from starting. This was separate from the earlier disabled Generate button issue, which had already been fixed.

We have deployed redundant moderation using two independent services, Cloudflare Clef and TypeSafe Jev, in parallel. A rejection from either service blocks generation. If one service is slow or unavailable, generation proceeds on the other service's approval after a short wait, so a single service outage no longer blocks image generation. We also added diagnostics to investigate service availability and disagreements without recording user descriptions.

Build 9 explicitly names TypeSafe AI in the sharing notice and requires renewed consent before generation. The public privacy policy has also been updated.

We verified three-sheet generation on a physical iPhone 17 Pro running iOS 27.2, including device verification and consent. The updated backend also passed live generation checks, and unit and privacy UI tests passed on iPhone and iPad simulators.

To test:
1. Open Wonder Lines with an internet connection.
2. Enter "A friendly dinosaur riding a bicycle on the moon".
3. Tap Generate and then Agree and Generate on the AI sharing notice.
4. Wait for the three sheets, then swipe or use the arrows to view them.

No sign-in, purchase or settings change is required. Generation is free for users, subject to service availability and a daily allowance of 100 images per anonymous account. Settings includes consent withdrawal, account and data deletion, the privacy policy, and support.
