Suggested addition to the next App Review notes, after deployment verification:

Wonder Lines can suspend abusive anonymous accounts from image generation.
Twenty confirmed content-policy rejections in a rolling 24 hours automatically
suspend generation for 24 hours. A description batch and its retries count once;
moderation service failures and uncertain results do not add strikes. Authorized
operators can also suspend or reinstate an account through private administration
controls. Suspension is enforced server-side, including existing access tokens
and queued work that has not started provider submission. Reporting, support and
account deletion remain available.

This is a draft for the next submission. It does not modify already submitted
App Store Connect review notes or assert that Apple has approved these controls.
