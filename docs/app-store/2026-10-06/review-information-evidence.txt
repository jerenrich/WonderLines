Please review Wonder Lines 1.0 (4), which includes the editing, saved-gallery, and interrupted-generation recovery improvements made since build 3. 1. Physical-device demonstration The recording in WonderLines-1.0-4-physical-device.zip was captured on a ninth-generation iPad running iPadOS 27.0.1. It begins with launching Wonder Lines and demonstrates settings, entering a description, generating real sheets through the live service, browsing results, Save to Photos, the system share sheet, print options, usage information, and editing while retaining the gallery. This uses the live app/service, with no mock images. Print options are demonstrated without sending a job to a printer. There is no user-facing registration, login, or profile. A random installation-specific service identity is created automatically for authentication/recovery; no name or email is requested. Data-removal requests can be made to jordan.erenrich@gmail.com as explained in the privacy policy. Prompts/sheets are private; there is no public gallery, feed, messaging, or access to other users' content. There are no social reporting or blocking flows. Descriptions are moderated before generation. Support and content concerns can be emailed to the same address. There are no paid features, purchases, subscriptions, ads, or paywalls. Usage & cost reports provider costs/estimates for transparency; it does not charge the user. 2. Purpose and audience Wonder Lines turns a text idea into printable AI-generated coloring sheets on iPhone and iPad. It serves people seeking creative coloring activities, including families, educators, and adult coloring enthusiasts. It avoids searching for a premade page or drawing one manually. Age difficulty guides simpler shapes or finer detail; it is a drawing setting, not age verification. Adults should supervise family use. 3. Setup and main features No login credentials, API keys, uploads, or sample files are needed. Internet access is required for new generation. Open Settings, choose an age difficulty (e.g. 6), keep Sunburst, and choose 1-3 images per generation. Return, enter “A friendly dinosaur riding a bicycle on the moon”, and tap Generate/the sparkle button. Wait for the sheets, then swipe or use the arrows to select one. Save to Photos requests add-only permission. Share uses the system sheet. Print requires an AirPrint-compatible printer. On compact iPhone layouts these actions are in Sheet actions. Settings also offers other models and Diagnostics. Check unfinished sheets recovers existing jobs without creating another batch. Generation is free, subject to availability and a maximum of 100 images per installation account per UTC day. 4. Services Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, R2, Images, and AI Gateway host and process generation. OpenAI supplies Flare/Sunburst generation; Cloudflare Workers AI supplies FLUX/Leonardo model options and Clef description moderation; fal.ai supplies ColoringBook.Redmond V2. Apple App Attest verifies devices, Keychain stores installation credentials, and native Photos/share/AirPrint APIs handle export. No advertising or analytics SDK is included. 5. Regions Features, models, content rules, and UTC limits are the same across regions. The interface is English. Availability of internet/provider services and local AirPrint equipment can affect access; there are no app-controlled regional feature variations. 6. Regulation and content rights This is a creative coloring utility, not a regulated service. It contains no licensed third-party character library, publications, or other protected content catalogue. Sheets are generated from user descriptions using provider accounts/models authorized for this use. No industry credentials apply. Listing screenshots used a supplied illustration in an isolated simulator capture; build 4 has no screenshot fixture. Privacy and support: https://jerenrich.github.io/WonderLines/privacy.html"
