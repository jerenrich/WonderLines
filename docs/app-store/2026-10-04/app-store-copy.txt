APP NAME
Wonder Lines: Coloring Sheets

SUBTITLE
Turn ideas into printable coloring pages

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
A dinosaur cycling on the moon? Describe an idea, choose the age difficulty, and create a coloring sheet to save, share, or print.

DESCRIPTION
Turn a big imagination into a page to color.

A dinosaur riding a bike on the moon. A friendly dragon in a garden. Whatever scene you have in mind, Wonder Lines turns your description into AI-generated coloring sheets on iPhone and iPad.

DESCRIBE YOUR IDEA
Type a character, scene, or story. Choose an age difficulty to guide the drawing toward simpler shapes and larger coloring areas, or finer details.

FIND YOUR FAVORITE
Create one to five sheets at a time. Swipe through the results and choose the page you want to use. Adjust your description or try another image model for your next set.

TAKE IT TO PAPER
Save a sheet to Photos, share it, or print it with a compatible AirPrint printer. Landscape pages give your ideas room to grow and keep the whole drawing visible in the preview.

MAKE SOMETHING TOGETHER
Use a child's favorite idea as the starting point for a quiet afternoon, a rainy-day activity, or a page to share with family.

No sign-in is required. An internet connection is needed to generate new sheets. Generation is subject to service availability and usage limits. AI-generated results vary with your description and chosen settings.

APP REVIEW NOTES
Wonder Lines creates AI-generated coloring sheets from text descriptions. No sign-in or review account is required; the app creates an anonymous service identity automatically.

To test:
1. Open Settings and choose an age difficulty, for example 6 years.
2. Set Images per generation to 1 and keep the default model.
3. Return to the main screen and enter: Dinosaur riding a bike on the moon.
4. Tap Generate (the sparkle button on compact iPhone layouts) and wait for the result.
5. Save to Photos, Share, or Print the selected sheet. On iPhone, these actions are in the Sheet actions menu at the top right.

New sheet generation requires an internet connection and the hosted generation service. Saving requests add-only Photos access. Printing requires a compatible AirPrint printer.

The listing screenshots were captured from the current interface in iOS simulators with a locally supplied dinosaur illustration. The submitted app generates its images through the live service; no screenshot fixture or special screenshot mode is included in the shipping source.
